# V0.2.0F.2 — Visual validation report

Date: 2026-10-18. Seed **310929**. Build: current working tree (uncommitted V0.2.0D – V0.2.0F.2).

## Method and its limits

- **Batch, reproducible captures**, not Game View. Play mode cannot be driven interactively from this environment. The
  harness (`Docs/Reports/V0.2.0F.2_ValidationCaptures.cs.txt`, run as an EditMode test in a copy of the project) builds
  the planet with the real `PlanetVisualController`, `AdaptiveTerrainRenderer` (adaptive LOD, floating origin),
  `PlanetExploreCamera`, atmosphere and cloud shells, and renders with the project's URP pipeline and shaders.
- Render target 960 x 540, MSAA 4x, **no post-processing** (Chromatic Aberration is therefore absent by construction;
  the user disabled it in Unity as well). Sun about 30 degrees above the horizon at each location.
- Not reproduced by the harness: Unity editor frame timing and input, the UI click path, the Game View's own post
  stack, temporal effects. These are listed as limits per scenario.
- Captures: `Docs/Captures/V0.2.0F.2_Validation/` (71 PNG). Metadata per capture (seed, clearance, camera position in
  planet space, LOD budget, material mode, debug view, note): `captures.md`. Raw numbers: `measurements.txt`.
- Image difference = mean absolute difference per channel on 0 .. 255; "% px" = pixels changing by more than 2/255.

## B — LOD stability — **PARTIAL**

Camera fixed, covering rebuilt with budgets 384, 512 and 1024 (384 -> 1023 patches).

| View | 10 m: 384 vs 1024 | 100 m: 384 vs 1024 | 100 m: 512 vs 1024 |
|---|---|---|---|
| PBR final lit | 6.02 (80.5 % px) | 6.40 (73.8 % px) | 4.73 (63.0 % px) |
| PBR albedo view (unlit) | 5.29 | 4.67 | 3.75 |
| Scale view (distance only) | 6.76 | 0.27 | 0.22 |
| Procedural mode, final lit (reference) | 8.44 | 5.03 | 3.44 |

(At 10 m the 384 and 512 budgets give the same image: the 512 budget does not add patches in that view.)

Observed (`B_*_budget*`, `B_*_diff_*`, `sheet_B_lod_100m.png`):
- The budget change **is a real geometry change**: the valley's shape visibly differs between 384 and 1024 patches
  (coarser relief, different silhouettes). Every view changes with it.
- The texture does **not drift independently of the geometry**: the albedo differences follow the mesh (facet-shaped
  areas, speckle where high-frequency texture moves with sub-pixel surface displacement). There is no global offset or
  scale jump; the Scale view is stable at 100 m (0.27).
- Remaining defect (minor, shader/material): per-vertex inputs (biome colour and cover, triplanar weights from the
  band-limited mesh normal, coarse-level geology) are interpolated over the displayed triangles, so a refinement
  changes material blends inside faceted areas. Comparable to the procedural mode (PBR 4.73–6.40 vs procedural
  3.44–5.03 at 100 m). The bulk of the difference is geometric LOD.

## D — Biomes and coastlines — **PARTIAL**

Locations found by the same rules as the earlier captures: grassland (680 m), crystalline mountain (about 20 km), flat
sandy coast (< 12 m). Each at 1.7 m, 10 m, 1 km and 300 km; Material ID, Tint, Scale and procedural reference at 10 m
and 1 km.

- **Grassland** (`sheet_D_grassland.png`, `D_grassland_*`): Material ID = grass; recognizable grass texture at 1.7 m and
  10 m (blades, twigs); smooth at 1 km; green region preserved from 300 km. Reads as a short, dry-ish lawn rather than
  lush grass (scan + biome tint). Minor.
- **Mountain** (`sheet_D_mountain.png`): rock texture readable at 1.7 m, 10 m, 1 km. **Defect (medium, shader):** on the
  very steep face seen almost edge-on at 1.7 m (`D_mountain_1_7m`, right side), the texture shows strong radial streaks
  — stretching / anisotropic minification of a triplanar projection at a grazing angle. Not seen on the other faces.
  At 300 km the region is mostly under clouds.
- **Coast** (`sheet_D_coast.png`): Material ID = sand over the whole beach, Tint view uniformly neutral (**no colour
  overlay**). Remaining: the beach is a large uniform surface with a broad specular sheen towards the sun (wet band
  smoothness); the waterline is a thin, sharp line. Classified as:
  - geometry: flat terrain meeting a flat sea (no shore profile, no swash zone) — terrain geometry, not material;
  - ocean intersection: thin line where the ocean sphere meets the terrain — ocean, not material;
  - material: sheen intensity of the wet band (minor).
- Unity post-processing: not involved in these captures (no post stack in the harness).
